## Notas para la certificación (en inglés, para los revisores)

```
DeskIsleBar is a WPF desktop app (full trust, runFullTrust) that shows a small floating "island" at the top of the screen.
- It reads the local Windows notification database (read-only) to show app notifications in the island. Nothing is sent anywhere.
- No account, no sign-in, no in-app purchases, no ads, no telemetry.
- Network: Open-Meteo for weather (only the city the user types) and the iCal calendar links the user adds. Updates come from the Store.
- To try it: hover the mouse over the black pill at the top of the screen; drag sideways to switch panels. A short welcome tour opens on first launch.
- "Start with Windows" uses the package StartupTask and is off until the user turns it on in Settings.
```

## English

**Name:** DeskIsleBar for Windows

**Description:**

```
DeskIsleBar is a floating island for Windows: a black pill at the edge of your screen that shows what matters without getting in the way. It expands when something happens and shrinks back when it's done.

• Music: cover, title, controls and progress for whatever is playing (Spotify, Tidal, Apple Music, YouTube Music and any app that uses the Windows media controls). The mouse wheel changes the volume.
• Notifications: your apps' notifications show up on the island with their icon, and one click opens the app or the chat. While you're using the island, they wait instead of interrupting you.
• Calendar: your upcoming Google Calendar or Outlook events, a month view and a reminder before each one.
• Weather, PC status (CPU, GPU, RAM and free up space), quick notes, timers and Pomodoro with focus mode.
• Useful alerts: low battery on controllers and Bluetooth headphones, camera or microphone in use (with indicators while it lasts), Caps Lock, internet down and more. Each one can be turned on or off.
• Stays out of your games: it hides in full screen and saves notifications for later.
• Lightweight: about 60 MB of RAM and well under 1% CPU.

Private: no accounts, no ads and no telemetry. It never asks for personal or payment data, and your notifications and settings never leave your PC.

In English and Spanish. Free and open source.
```
